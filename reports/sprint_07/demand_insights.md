# Sprint 7 – Exploratory Analysis and Demand Insights

## 1. Dataset Overview
- Total job postings: 498
- Unique job titles: 10
- Countries represented: 43
- Jobs with specified skills: 393
- Jobs with salary information: 15

## 2. Job Role Insights
- Software Engineer has the highest demand with 173 postings.
- Data Scientist follows with 135 postings.
- Data Engineer has 44 postings.
- Data Analyst has 12 postings.

## 3. Geographic Insights
- India leads with 115 job postings.
- United States follows with 60 postings.
- China has 46 postings.
- The dataset represents 43 countries.

## 4. Work Arrangement Insights
- 47 postings (9.44%) are explicitly marked as work from home.
- 451 postings (90.56%) are not marked as work from home.
- Not marked as work from home does not necessarily mean office-based.

## 5. Employment Type Insights
- Full-time: 482 mentions
- Internship: 13 mentions
- Unspecified: 2 mentions
- Contractor: 1 mention

Note: These figures represent employment-type mentions after splitting the schedule-type field, not necessarily unique job postings.

## 6. Salary Insights
- Only 15 postings contain valid salary information.
- Average annual standardized salary: 136269.33
- Median annual standardized salary: 150000
- Salary findings should be interpreted cautiously because of limited coverage and international currency differences.
Note: Demand percentages are calculated using 498 total job postings as the denominator. A posting can mention multiple skills, so percentages do not need to add up to 100%.

## 7. Skill Demand Insights
Top five skills:
1. Microsoft Azure – 243 postings
2. Python – 175 postings
3. SQL – 148 postings
4. C# – 141 postings
5. Java – 104 postings

Microsoft Azure has the highest demand among the extracted skills.

## 8. Weekly Skill Trends
- Weekly demand trends were analyzed for the top five skills.
- 52 weeks of data were included.
- 260 skill-week records were generated.
- Zero-demand weeks were included to ensure complete trend analysis.

## 9. Key Conclusions
- Software Engineering and Data Science are the most frequently advertised roles.
- Microsoft Azure, Python, and SQL are important skills in the dataset.
- India has the highest number of postings.
- Salary analysis is limited by the small number of salary records.
- Weekly skill trends provide historical demand patterns that can support future forecasting.

## 10. Sprint 7 Outcome
Exploratory analysis and visualizations were completed to identify job role demand, geographic distribution, employment patterns, salary distribution, and skill demand trends.
